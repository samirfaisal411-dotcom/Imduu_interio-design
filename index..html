ABOUT IMDUU

Thoughtful design,
built for Mumbai homes

We started Imduu Interior Design with a simple belief — a home should be planned around the people who live in it, not around a catalogue. Our designers spend time understanding how a family moves through their space before a single material is chosen, so the layout, storage and finishes all work for daily life, not just for photographs.

From compact Mumbai apartments to larger villas, we manage design, execution and after-handover support as one continuous process, so you deal with one team from the first conversation to the day you move in.

HOW WE WORK

From first conversation to move-in day

01

Consultation & Concept

We visit your space, understand your budget and lifestyle, and put together a concept tailored to how you'll actually use each room.

02

Design & Planning

Layouts, material boards and cost estimates are finalised together with you, so there are no surprises once work begins.

03

3D Visualization

Walk through your home before it's built. We fine-tune colours, finishes and layout based on how the 3D render actually feels.

04

Installation & Handover

Our execution team completes the work within the agreed timeline, with a final walkthrough before we hand over the keys.

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Minimal or maximal — designed with the same care

Calm & minimal

Clean lines, multi-functional furniture and a quiet palette for homes that need to breathe.

Bold & layered

Rich textures, statement lighting and considered colour for homes that like to make an impression.

Somewhere in between

Most homes sit between the two — we mix elements from both so the result feels entirely yours.
